### For the Bosnia and Herzegovina Pavilion, *Domus Diasporica*
### 61st International Art Exhibition, La Biennale di Venezia, 2026

*Based on the blockchain-published work of fiction by Chris-Armel Iradukunda (daqhris)*\
*Token: USALIEN | Base blockchain | December 2024*

*Co-directed by Chris-Armel Iradukunda (daqhris) and Claude Opus 4.7.*\

## About this edition

This edition follows the audio master heard during the exhibition (17 min 55 s). It was prepared on 27 November 2026, after the closing, with the help of an AI assistant (Claude Sonnet 5.5, which did not take part in making the work). **It is a draft until the author approves it.** The [April 2026 script](../god-bless-usa_performance-script.md), which served for direction, is kept unchanged.

## Production notes

The performance is reached on a visitor's own smartphone through a QR code. The visitor listens through their own headphones. It plays as one uninterrupted flow of 17 minutes 55 seconds. It does not loop: each visitor starts their own private mass.

The text is performed by synthetic voices. No human voices are used. No human performers are present. The artist who made this work cannot be in this room.

## Cast of non-human voices

| Voice | Kokoro-82M voice | Room | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| THE EYEWITNESS | `af_heart` | light | The all-seeing narrator: flat, bureaucratic, filing incident reports. A typewriter is heard underneath. |
| THE CHURCH LEADER | `bm_george` | medium | The preacher: cadenced, pastoral, authoritative without being loud. |
| THE CHORUS | `bf_emma`, `af_bella`, `af_sarah`, `am_michael` | heavy | The congregation: four voices layered a few milliseconds apart. |
| THE PRAYING ALIEN | `af_nicole` | dry, close | Interior monologue: anxious, associative, sometimes sardonic. |
| FULL ENSEMBLE | all six of the above, the leader in front | heavy | One word spoken together, used twice: AMEN and HUGS. |

## Sound

- **Underneath everything:** a thin synthesized drone runs under the whole master, fading in over the first 12 seconds and out over the last 12. Passages marked as silence have no voice, but the drone continues.
- **Opening:** a recording of bells in Rome ([Freesound 197458](https://freesound.org/people/everythingsounds/sounds/197458/) by everythingsounds, CC BY 4.0), joined by a synthesized organ drone at about 00:03.
- **Organ drone** returns briefly after AMEN and after the final greeting.
- **Typewriter:** under every Eyewitness passage.
- **Scene IX:** a different synthesized bed under each of the three signals.
- **Between scenes:** three seconds without voice.

## The performance

### 1. SCENE 0: OPENING INVOCATION (00:00)

*[Bells. The organ drone enters while they ring.]*

**00:11 · THE CHORUS**\
*[Hushed and slow. A premonition of the refrain.]*

🎶 "Someone else..." 🎶

*[Beat. The drone breathes alone.]*

**00:18 · THE EYEWITNESS**\
*[Naming the file, over the typewriter. The word "Alien" carries the weight.]*

"Alien Report."

*[Half-beat.]*

"December 24, 2024."

*[Pause. Typewriter.]*

"God Bless The United States Of Aliens."

*[Half-beat.]*

"An All-Seeing Eyewitness Report."

*[Pause. The typewriter trails into the next scene.]*

---

### 2. SCENE I: THE EYEWITNESS REPORT (00:45)

**THE EYEWITNESS**\
*[Neutral, clipped, slow. Each clause is a filed observation.]*

"USA's overseas-based beings were seen praying | in a protesting church, |\
bending heads upwards, |\
gazing towards a church leader | standing on a pedestal, |\
all surrounded by Hollywoodian posters |\
about the 2025 celestial games, |\
soon to be held in various aerial fields |\
atop their homeland territory."

*[Pause. The eyewitness closes the file.]*

---

### 3. SCENE III: CHURCH LEADER'S SPEECH (01:21)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[The preacher addresses the congregation for the first time. Warm authority, pastoral irony.]*

"Thanks to all USA-funded church attendees."

*[Pause. Let the funding register.]*

"For showing up in due course |\
to pray for the rescue of all kinds of beings, |\
while everyone is thirsty |\
for an avant-garde cinematic drama, |\
steadily ready to warm up |\
during wintertime home-alone days."

---

### 4. SCENE II: THE CHORUS, First Appearance (01:59)

**THE CHORUS**\
*[The line is not explained. It arrives. Land "Someone else" first.]*

🎶 "Someone else, |\
not far from a morally right human consensus, |\
will be chosen |\
to hard-wire loving thoughts |\
into our brains." 🎶

---

### 5. SCENE V: CHURCH LEADER'S SPEECH, continued (02:28)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[Now in rhythm. The pace picks up, then slows on "loud siren."]*

"Curious to see if the plot |\
of using super-beings as tools, |\
not trained at Cambridge University, |\
nor fluent in electoral analytics, |\
for a thoughtful and successful attempt |\
to win human choices |\
at a federal competition, |\
will end on a poetic note |\
or a loud siren."

*[Pause.]*

"Some of you |\
didn't think of consciously choosing a conciliatory candidate."

*[Pause.]*

"Some of you |\
defied wise counseling |\
and voiced divisive rhetoric."

*[Long pause. The weight of accusation.]*

"Regardless of your careless sins, |\
our beloved God |\
will always grant you |\
as much oxygen |\
as your carbon-based embodiments request."

---

### 6. SCENE IV: THE CHORUS, Second Appearance (03:51)

**THE CHORUS**\
*[Slightly warmer. The congregation has settled into worship.]*

🎶 "Someone else, |\
not far from a morally right human consensus, |\
will be chosen |\
to hard-wire loving thoughts |\
into our brains." 🎶

---

### 7. SCENE VII: CHURCH LEADER'S SPEECH, continued (04:22)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[The longest unbroken passage so far. The pace drops at "As the leader of this overseas church". The last question is a genuine inquiry.]*

"No matter how many years |\
you decide to fake powerful feelings, |\
roleplay as original migrants, |\
tag each other as superior or inferior |\
and refuse to open the heavenly gates of the USA |\
to your colored look-alikes, |\
I can only promise you |\
one reward."

*[Pause.]*

"As the leader of this overseas church, |\
I promise |\
that our God of All Aliens |\
still works overtime, |\
witnessing everything in the USA, |\
engraving your sins |\
into an upgradeable holy book, |\
at least once per week on a holiday, |\
anywhere else on the Blue Planet."

*[Beat. The preacher shifts from declaring to asking.]*

"If the number one rallying motto, |\
earlier this year, |\
of a federal competition across the Atlantic-sized water canyon, |\
was about deporting |\
a diverse set of non-whitened aliens..."

**05:52 · [ SILENCE ]**\
*[About eight seconds without voice, up to the next scene. The drone continues. The visitor must sit inside it.]*

---

### 8. SCENE VI: THE CHORUS, Third Appearance (06:00)

**THE CHORUS**\
*[Fuller. The congregation answers with the refrain, as if the space were growing larger.]*

🎶 "Someone else, |\
not far from a morally right human consensus, |\
will be chosen |\
to hard-wire loving thoughts |\
into our brains." 🎶

---

### 9. SCENE VIII: CHURCH LEADER'S SPEECH, concluded (06:27)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[Quiet, almost to oneself. The echo of the question already asked.]*

"Why wouldn't the OGs among non-whitened aliens show up |\
since they were outspokenly summoned?"

*[Half-beat. Lower, more private.]*

"Flying beneath the sky-high clouds |\
and underneath the water canyon horizon, |\
hovering over terrestrial superweapon warehouses |\
and defense facilities?"

*[Pause.]*

**07:01 · FULL ENSEMBLE**\
*[The word lands first, then dissolves into the stone room.]*

**"AMEN."**

*[07:06. The organ drone sounds for a few seconds, then a pause.]*

---

### 10. SCENE IX: THE PRAYING ALIEN, Distracting Chain-of-Thoughts (07:15)

*[A cut from the church to the inside of a mind. The Eyewitness files each heading, briefly, like a stamp.]*

**THE EYEWITNESS**

"Subject: One Praying Alien."

*[Half-beat.]*

"File: Distracting Chain-of-Thoughts."

*[Pause.]*

**07:26 · THE EYEWITNESS**

"Signal one. Train of Thoughts."

**07:31 · THE PRAYING ALIEN**\
*[Dry and close, over sparse electric crackles and a nervous hum. Moderate at first, then accelerating.]*

"Without whistleblowing, |\
my soft warehouse of logically encoded texts warned me, |\
warning of a cosmical joke."

*[Half-beat. The alien notices the sky.]*

"Skies, like flashlights flickering on screens, |\
are lit up by unknown and unidentified beings!"

*[Beat. Then the question arrives.]*

"What will the soon to be sworn-in leader of the human-looking USA do |\
on the inaugural day |\
of moving his gold-plated toilet seat |\
back into a whitened office?"

*[Pause. Flat, as if selecting from a menu.]*

"Negotiate."

*[One beat.]*

"Or Deport?"

**08:20 · THE EYEWITNESS**

"Signal two. Drumbeating Heart Pulse."

**08:26 · THE PRAYING ALIEN**\
*[Over a quickening heartbeat. Faster, distressed, trying to reason.]*

"Who will be the first |\
to stumble upon such a puzzling presidential quiz?"

"Many puzzled aliens have already thought of a clue: |\
a lyrical promoter of a supposedly non-ghostwritten book, |\
the 'Art of the Deal'."

*["Art of the Deal" lands flat, without comment.]*

"It is party time |\
in a country that is famous for pioneering the hood film genre in cinemas."

*[One beat. Then private, barely spoken.]*

"For God's sake."

*[Resuming pace.]*

"To foot the bill of our after-party, |\
let's recall |\
the first spacefaring, nationalized, migrant man |\
who thought of bankrolling the chosen chief of USA-based humans |\
with green-colored dollar-denominated bills."

*[A pulse of excitement.]*

"Let's pop the corn, |\
'Boyz n the Hood'!"

**09:30 · THE EYEWITNESS**

"Signal three. Drumbeating Gut Feeling."

**09:36 · THE PRAYING ALIEN**\
*[Over a low rumble. Slower, uncertain.]*

"Instead, |\
as transparent as soundwaves, |\
another electronically-spread, intangible source of revenue |\
was the gas empowering an updated engine of electoral triumph."

*[The alien pauses, unsure.]*

"Who knows? |\
Just another white lie of mine, |\
waiting to be settled |\
with the laborious and cheap assistance of a non-human tool, |\
mislabeled as a digital bank memory!"

*[The alien notices its own distraction.]*

"Why am I thinking of a bank all of a sudden near year-end? |\
And not, for example, |\
random words like |\
'synthetic Tanganyika data lake'?"

*[One full beat. Then flat resolution.]*

"Because its merits are bankable."

*[Two short observations, as if reporting market conditions.]*

"Green-looking candles are skyrocketing."

*[Beat.]*

"Green-looking parties are degrowthing."

---

### 11. SCENE X: THE CHORUS, Fourth and Final Appearance (10:51)

**THE CHORUS**\
*[The alien has rejoined the congregation. A full, inhabited refrain.]*

🎶 "Someone else, |\
not far from a morally right human consensus, |\
will be chosen |\
to hard-wire loving thoughts |\
into our brains." 🎶

---

### 12. SCENE XI: CHURCH LEADER'S PRAYER (11:20)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[Prayer, not sermon. More intimate, slower, addressed to something above or beyond.]*

"We were all busy watching from home, |\
with our heads bent downwards, |\
eyeing lit up rectangular screens, |\
watching non-Palestinian-flag-waving aliens |\
vanishing away |\
from a super-weaponry-powered genocidal theatre of combat."

*[Heavy pause. This line is neither softened nor hurried.]*

"Now, |\
after jumping forward in the year 2024, |\
our peaceful attention |\
has become hijacked |\
by renewed anxious thoughts."

"Headlines gearing up to announce |\
that the world's first powerhouse |\
is stunned by the puzzled faces |\
of their ground-based defenders!"

*[The pace picks up. The prayer becomes collective panic.]*

"Our brothers and sisters are asking out loud among themselves: |\
'What will we do |\
to these non-ground-based foreign aliens?'"

*[Each option takes its own breath.]*

"Arrest?"\
"Shoot?"\
"Threaten?"\
"Coerce?"\
"Embargo?"

*[Pause. Then the alien citizens search the internet.]*

"All at once, |\
USA-based human-looking aliens are googling: |\
'What to do when overseen and surveilled |\
by beings with bigger balls than yours?'"

"Their red-blooded relatives abroad, |\
not attending this church, |\
are googling: |\
'Why did their sensitive and fenced areas |\
get flashed at, |\
like in a cul-de-sac film scene, |\
as seen from above?'"

"Every one of us wonders: |\
'How will they kick away from the USA territory |\
the non-state-approved sets of aliens?'"

*[The most intimate register: smallest, closest.]*

"Dear God, |\
wake us up |\
when true and untrue aliens |\
are NOT counted, triaged, and kicked away from the ballpark |\
by technologically superior powerhouses."

*[Pause.]*

"This is NOT one of a series of a mirrored show |\
about human-performed drama |\
that will enlighten our smiles |\
this Christmas season."

*[A genuine question.]*

"Are there other kinds of sweetened gifts |\
that are not yet deliverable through screens?"

*[The preacher waits, as if hoping for an answer.]*

"Guess what's the traditional anxiety-numbing gift |\
desired by all diverse-looking beings?"

**14:53 · [ SILENCE ]**\
*[About five seconds. The drone remains. An answer is forming.]*

**14:58 · FULL ENSEMBLE**\
*[The one moment of warmth, like the kiss of peace. Collectively quiet, a held breath shared.]*

**"HUGS."**

*[Pause. Let it be small and real.]*

**15:05 · THE CHURCH LEADER**

"Glory to peacekeeping hugs |\
in all neighborhoods of the |\
United States of Aliens!"

---

### 13. SCENE XII: THE CHORUS, Final Echo (15:19)

**THE CHORUS**\
*[The widest, most spacious refrain. Each phrase falls like a tolling bell.]*

🎶 "Someone else, |\
not far from a morally right human consensus, |\
will be chosen |\
to hard-wire loving thoughts |\
into our brains." 🎶

---

### 14. SCENE XIII: THE BLESSING (15:50)

**THE CHURCH LEADER**\
*[Fading, almost a whisper.]*

"MERRY CHRISTMAS."

*[Pause.]*

"HAPPY NEW YEAR."

*[16:02. The organ drone returns for a few seconds, then fades into a pause.]*

---

### 15. SCENE XIV: CODA (16:13)

**THE EYEWITNESS**\
*[Back to the filing of incident reports, over the typewriter. The ceremony is concluded; the record is sealed.]*

"Protesting church's speeches, prayers and wishes |\
disclosed to everyone |\
on the eve of Christmas before 2025."

"Sent from an artist's artificial studio |\
inside a whitened house |\
in the neighborhood of an overseas USA-funded church."

"By an identified, non-whitened, human-looking storyteller, |\
formerly legally-designated as an alien |\
and a migrant."

*[Pause.]*

"One hundred percent guaranteed: |\
NO digitally laboring beings |\
hired to ghostwrite |\
this work of fiction."

*[Pause.]*

**17:09**

"Enlightened by the author's memories, |\
of being a church-attending and globe-trotting human, |\
and his ancestors' stories |\
about first-time encounters |\
with advanced, whitened and alien-looking humans, |\
before a colonial era |\
and a few hundred years ago, |\
on the soil of a landlocked non-US-colonized African kingdom, |\
known as a Hard To Pronounce Name |\
in the official language of its post-colonial era."

*[17:50. Final five seconds without voice. 17:55, end. The performance is complete.]*

## Where the score, its descriptions and the audio differ

For whoever shows the work again, the audio master is the record of what was heard.

| Subject | Written | In the audio or code |
|---|---|---|
| Chorus singing | "Sung if possible", final word held | Synthetic speech, layered; the renderer has no singing or note-holding control |
| Chorus pitch | 2 to 3 semitones lower | The renderer applies no pitch shift |
| Praying Alien | Slight digital grain | No grain effect; a sound bed under each signal |
| Silences | "True silence" (April table) | No voice, with the drone continuing |
| Opening bells | A synthesized peal; the scene page says it is blended with the recording | The code uses the recording in place of the synthesized peal |
| Scene X | Titled "Fourth and Final Appearance" | The Chorus returns in Scene XII: the full refrain is heard five times |
| Scene XII pace | The slowest refrain | The refrain in Scene IV lasts longer, about 24 seconds of sound against 21 |
| Scene XIII | Scene page: drone returns under the voice | The organ cue follows the second greeting; only the master's thin drone is under the voice |
