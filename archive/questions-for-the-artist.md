## "First" claims

Do not state that this was the first showing by a person of Burundian origin at
the Venice Biennale. The
[2015 South African Pavilion catalogue](https://sapavilion.partsandlabour.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/PSA_Catalogue-What-remains-is-tomorrow.pdf)
includes Serge Alain Nitegeka, born 1983, in Burundi according to the catalogue
(his [gallery biography](https://www.stevenson.info/artist/serge-alain-nitegeka/biography)
says Rwanda). A narrower claim, for instance about a Burundian-born artist
co-directing a sound performance with an AI model there, was neither confirmed nor
disproved; it needs research by someone with access to the Biennale's historical
archive ([ASAC](https://www.labiennale.org/en/asac)) before use.

Wording that needs no such research: "A guest work by Burundian-born artist
Chris-Armel Iradukunda (daqhris), co-directed with an AI model, presented in the
Pavilion of Bosnia and Herzegovina."
